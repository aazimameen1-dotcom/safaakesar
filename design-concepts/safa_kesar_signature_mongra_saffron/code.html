The Pampore Origin expand_more

Our Signature Mongra is grown exclusively in the karewas (highlands) of Pampore, Kashmir. Known globally as the 'Saffron Town', the unique loamy soil and crisp autumnal air of this region produce crocus flowers with exceptionally long, deep crimson stigmas.

We hand-pick and carefully separate only the thickest, darkest red tips (the Mongra) to ensure maximum flavor, aroma (safranal), and coloring strength (crocin). No yellow style, no floral waste—just pure, unadulterated saffron.
