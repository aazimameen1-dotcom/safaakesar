## Brand & Style

The design system for this premium Kashmiri saffron brand is built on the philosophy of **Provenance, Purity, and Proof**. It avoids the artificial "royal luxury" tropes common in the industry, opting instead for a **Corporate Modern** foundation infused with **Minimalist** and **Tactile** elements. The visual language is calm, unhurried, and grounded in the specific landscape of Pampore.

The aesthetic prioritizes clarity and evidentiary detail over decorative flourishes. High-quality macro photography of physical product textures—the deep crimson of Mongra threads and the matte finish of walnut shells—serves as the primary visual interest. The interface should feel like a well-documented botanical record: precise, authentic, and undeniably real.

**Key Brand Pillars:**
- **Calm Authority:** No aggressive sales banners or artificial urgency.
- **Radical Transparency:** Clear labeling of batch numbers, harvest dates, and origin coordinates.
- **Human Connection:** Highlighting the physical storefront and named staff (e.g., Aadil) to bridge the gap between a remote digital transaction and a local physical experience.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the saffron product and its agricultural environment rather than generic luxury symbols.

- **Warm Ivory (#F8F3EC)**: Acts as the primary canvas, providing a natural, paper-like warmth that feels more approachable and "organic" than pure white.
- **Deep Saffron-Red (#A6321E)**: The primary brand color. It represents the actual dried Mongra saffron threads. Use this for key actions and headers.
- **Saffron-Gold (#C68A2E)**: A secondary accent representing the saffron stigma and dried apricots. Use for highlights and secondary UI elements.
- **Walnut Ink (#2A241C)**: The primary text color. It provides high contrast against the ivory background while maintaining a softer, more natural feel than pure black.
- **Trust Olive (#5C6B4A)**: Reserved strictly for "Proof" elements: lab certifications, batch transparency labels, and authenticity badges. This distinguishes trust signals from marketing accents.
- **Crocus Purple (#6B4C7A)**: Use sparingly as a seasonal accent or for educational content regarding the flower/harvest, preventing it from overwhelming the product-focused red.

## Typography

The typography system pairs the literary, expressive qualities of **Fraunces** with the industrial precision of **Work Sans**.

- **Headlines (Fraunces):** Used for storytelling, product names, and section headers. Its soft curves and varied weights evoke a sense of heritage and unhurried quality.
- **Body & Metadata (Work Sans):** Used for technical details, shipping information, and instructions. It conveys reliability and straightforwardness.
- **Numerical Data:** Pricing and weight measurements must use **Work Sans** with tabular figures enabled. Saffron pricing is precise (to the gram), and the typography must reflect that exactness. Avoid any decorative treatment for prices; they should be clear, scannable, and transparent.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a **Fixed Grid** model on desktop to maintain a feeling of curated, editorial space. Content should never feel cramped; ample margins reflect the "unhurried" nature of the brand.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** A 12-column grid with generous 64px outer margins. Content is centered with a max-width of 1280px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with 32px margins.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column grid with 16px margins. 

Spacing follows an 8px base unit. Use larger vertical spacing (64px+) between sections to allow the eye to rest, emphasizing the calm atmosphere. Product galleries should use a "masonry-lite" or staggered approach to avoid the sterile look of perfectly uniform e-commerce grids.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain the grounded, "Provenance" focused aesthetic, this design system avoids heavy shadows and floating elements.

- **Tonal Layering:** Depth is primarily created through subtle shifts in surface color. Use slightly darker or lighter variations of Warm Ivory to define content blocks.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Use thin (1px) borders in a muted version of Walnut Ink or Trust Olive to define cards and input fields. 
- **The "Pressed" Effect:** Instead of shadows that make elements float "above" the page, use subtle inner borders or tonal shifts to make elements feel "inset" into the paper-like background, suggesting a tactile, physical quality.
- **Glassmorphism:** Use only for the "Live Order Mode" or "Harvest Status" indicators to give them a modern, "live" feel without detaching them from the grounded base.

## Shapes

The shape language is **Soft (0.25rem)**. This subtle rounding removes the clinical sharpness of square corners while avoiding the overly "bubbly" or "techy" look of fully rounded UI.

- **Buttons:** Use a consistent 0.25rem (4px) radius.
- **Product Cards:** Use 0.5rem (8px) for larger surface areas.
- **Packaging Labels:** Use sharp corners for certain "Proof" elements (like batch stickers) to mimic the look of physical printed labels applied to a product.

## Components

- **Buttons:** Primary buttons use the Saffron-Red background with Ivory text. Secondary buttons use a Walnut Ink outline. All buttons should have a slight "active" state that darkens the color rather than adding a shadow.
- **Trust Badges:** These are the most critical components. They should look like official stamps or labels, utilizing **Trust Olive** and **Label-Caps** typography. They should explicitly state data: "Batch #882," "Tested Jan 2024."
- **Weight/Price Calculator:** A custom interactive slider or button-group for selecting grams (0.5g, 1g, 5g, etc.). The price updates instantly using tabular figures to emphasize transparency.
- **Input Fields:** Minimalist design with a 1px Walnut Ink bottom border. Labels should use the **Label-Caps** style.
- **Harvest Banner:** A thin, persistent bar at the top of the site that changes based on the season. During October-November, it turns Saffron-Gold to signal the fresh harvest.
- **WhatsApp Direct:** A floating but unobtrusive button in the bottom right, styled in Trust Olive to separate it from marketing actions, emphasizing it as a direct line for authentic human support.
